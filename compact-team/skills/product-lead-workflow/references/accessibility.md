# Design accessibility assessment

Use to specify or assess accessible interaction, content, and motion behavior in product/design artifacts.

Check hierarchy, consistency, readability, contrast, focus visibility/order, target ergonomics, non-color feedback, labels, input errors, text scaling, and reduced motion. Verify applicable numeric accessibility/platform requirements against current authoritative guidance before claiming compliance. An annotated design is not a tested accessible implementation.

Consider touch targets/spacing, keyboard occlusion, safe areas, screen-reader order, longer localized strings, directional layouts, keyboard/pointer use, and reduced-motion needs as the target requires. Deliver testable requirements, acceptance checks, evidence, and remaining implementation/runtime validation gaps.

Authoritative reference: [W3C accessibility](https://www.w3.org/WAI/standards-guidelines/wcag/). Verify numeric/platform requirements against the actual target; do not treat a design annotation as an implementation test.
